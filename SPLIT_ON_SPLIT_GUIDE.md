# Split-on-Split Feature Guide

## What is Split-on-Split?

Split-on-split allows you to fairly divide receipt items based on **who ate what** and **how much they ate**.

---

## How It Works

### Example Scenario:
You have 2 food items:
- **Pizza (RM 30)** - Friend A ate the whole thing himself
- **Pasta (RM 20)** - Friend B and Friend C shared it equally

### Old Way (Unfair):
- Everyone pays RM 16.67 (RM 50 ÷ 3)
- ❌ Friend A pays too little (he ate RM 30 worth)
- ❌ Friends B & C pay too much (they only ate RM 10 each)

### New Way (Fair - Split-on-Split):
**Pizza:**
- Assign: Friend A
- Portions: Friend A gets **1×**
- Result: Friend A pays **RM 30** (100% of pizza)

**Pasta:**
- Assign: Friend B, Friend C
- Portions: Friend B gets **1×**, Friend C gets **1×**
- Result: Each pays **RM 10** (50% each)

**Final Split:**
- Friend A: RM 30
- Friend B: RM 10
- Friend C: RM 10
- ✅ Total: RM 50 (Fair!)

---

## Using the Feature

### Step 1: Assign Friends to Items
Click on friends who ate that item. They'll turn blue.

### Step 2: Set Portions
Use the **+** and **−** buttons to set how many portions each friend ate.

**Examples:**
- **Equal split (2 people)**: Both get **1×**
- **One person ate it all**: Give them **1×**, remove others
- **Unequal split**: Person A gets **2×**, Person B gets **1×** (A ate twice as much)

### Step 3: Review Summary
The "Split Summary" shows exactly what each person owes.

## Real-World Examples

### Scenario 1: Shared Appetizer + Individual Mains
- **Appetizer (RM 15)** - 3 friends shared
  - Friend A: 1×, Friend B: 1×, Friend C: 1×
  - Each pays: RM 5
  
- **Steak (RM 40)** - Friend A only
  - Friend A: 1×
  - Friend A pays: RM 40
  
- **Burger (RM 25)** - Friend B only
  - Friend B: 1×
  - Friend B pays: RM 25

**Result:**
- Friend A: RM 45 (appetizer + steak)
- Friend B: RM 30 (appetizer + burger)
- Friend C: RM 5 (appetizer only)

### Scenario 2: One Person Ate More
- **Pizza (RM 40)** - Friend A ate 3 slices, Friend B ate 1 slice
  - Friend A: 3×
  - Friend B: 1×
  - Total portions: 4
  - Friend A pays: RM 30 (3/4)
  - Friend B pays: RM 10 (1/4)

---

## Tips

💡 **Default behavior**: When you assign friends, they each get 1× by default (equal split)

💡 **Remove someone**: Click their blue button again to unassign them

💡 **You're included**: You're automatically included in splits with your friends

💡 **Portions are flexible**: Use any numbers that represent the ratio (2× and 1× = same as 4× and 2×)
